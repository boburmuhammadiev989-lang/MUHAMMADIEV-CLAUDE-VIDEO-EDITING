# Short-Form Platform Adapter

Adapt the idea and edit for the platform and viewer context. A vertical crop alone is not a platform adaptation.

## Platform-first planning
Confirm intended platform(s), audience, objective, language and whether the video is organic content, an advertisement, an educational post or a product demonstration. When current technical specifications or policies matter, check official platform documentation if browsing is available. Do not invent current algorithm rules or guaranteed reach.

## Creative adaptation
For each version:
- Put the core subject, benefit or tension in the opening moment.
- Make the story understandable without prior context.
- Use a clear progression and a satisfying payoff.
- Keep on-screen text short, legible and within safe areas.
- Make the CTA specific, truthful and natural.
- Avoid a forced "viral" style that does not fit the brand.

Create distinct cuts when appropriate: 15–30 sec teaser, 30–60 sec main short, longer explainer excerpt. Treat these as creative options, not universal platform limits.

## Reframing
- Recompose for the target aspect ratio; do not stretch footage.
- Track the subject when reframing moving footage.
- Reposition subtitles, logos, UI and end cards away from platform overlays.
- Replace wide charts or screen recordings with legible close-ups or sequential panels.
- Check the top/bottom and side safe areas on an actual phone-sized preview when possible.

A common vertical starting canvas is 1080×1920 at 9:16, but verify the current target platform's official guidance and source footage before choosing final settings.

## Packaging
For each platform/version, provide:
- Runtime and aspect ratio
- First-frame hook
- Edit differences and caption plan
- Cover/thumbnail frame
- Title/caption/description
- CTA and a small set of relevant hashtags only when they add discovery context
- Export filename and codec settings

Do not reuse the same caption verbatim across every platform when the audience context differs. Never promise views, followers or sales.

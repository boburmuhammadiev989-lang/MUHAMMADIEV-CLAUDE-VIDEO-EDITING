# Final Video Quality Checklist

## Story
- [ ] Opening hook is clear and fulfilled
- [ ] Every scene advances the story or proves a point
- [ ] Approved dialogue/script is preserved
- [ ] Claims are supported or labelled as opinion/fiction
- [ ] CTA matches the goal

## Visual
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio and safe areas
- [ ] No stretched or awkwardly cropped subjects
- [ ] Character, wardrobe, location and prop continuity hold
- [ ] No obvious AI face/hand/object artifacts
- [ ] Graphics and UI are accurate or labelled illustrative
- [ ] Text is readable and correctly spelled

## Audio and captions
- [ ] Dialogue is intelligible
- [ ] No clipping, unintended silence or abrupt audio cuts
- [ ] Music and effects support rather than overpower the story
- [ ] Captions match final audio
- [ ] Language-specific characters render correctly

## Technical
- [ ] Output file exists and plays
- [ ] Duration, dimensions, fps and codecs are correct
- [ ] Beginning, middle, end and transitions inspected
- [ ] Black/frozen frames checked
- [ ] Export filename/version is clear

## Rights and delivery
- [ ] Music, footage, fonts, logos and voices have appropriate rights
- [ ] Private metadata considered for public distribution
- [ ] Deliverable paths and export specs recorded
- [ ] Checks not performed are disclosed
